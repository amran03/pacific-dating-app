1. Muhtasari wa Mradi

Pacific Dating App ni programu ya kuchumbiana iliyojengwa kwa Flutter na Supabase kama backend. Inaunga mkono usajili wa watumiaji, kugundua watu wengine kwa eneo (GPS), kupenda/kukataa (swipe), kulinganisha (matching), gumzo la wakati halisi, kutuma zawadi kwa coins, simu za sauti na video (WebRTC), na kununua coins kupitia PesaPal.

3. Tabaka za Usanifu (Architecture Layers)

Programu ina tabaka tano. Kila tabaka linawasiliana tu na lile lililo chini yake, kwa hiyo mabadiliko kwenye database hayavunji UI.

#TabakaMahaliKazi
1 UI / Presentation lib/features/*/presentation/, lib/screens/ Widgja za Flutter zinazomwonyesha mtumiaji. Zinatumia setState na ChangeNotifier (kwa lugha/mandhari). Hazina SQL wala HTTP.
2 Services / Logic lib/core/services/, lib/services/ Biashara-logic yote: matchmaking, malipo, simu, arifa, presence. Hapa ndipo matchmaking_service.dart (24.8 KB) na notification_service.dart (16.0 KB) zinapokaa.
3 Data / Models lib/features/*/data/, lib/features/*/domain/models/ Madaraja ya data: UserModel (fields 43), ChatModel, GiftModel. Zinabadilisha JSON ya Supabase kuwa aina salama za Dart.
4 Backend (Supabase) supabase/migrations/, supabase/functions/ PostgreSQL yenye Row Level Security, Edge Functions za Deno za malipo ya PesaPal, Realtime, na Storage buckets.
5 Platform android/, ios/, n.k. Usanidi wa OS: ruhusa (camera, mic, location), signing, aikoni, manifest.

3.2 Kanuni za usanifu zilizofuatwa

  • Feature-first: kila kipengele kina folda yake; hakuna "god folder" ya screens.
  • Single source of truth: Supabase ndio chanzo kikuu; kifaa kinahifadhi tu mapendeleo (lugha, theme, token) kwa SharedPreferences.
  • Usalama wa upande wa server: bei ya coins na TZS huhesabiwa kwenye Edge Function, kamwe kwenye simu — ili mtumiaji asibadilishe bei.
  • Idempotent: pesapal_credit_coins() inaweza kuitwa mara nyingi bila kutoa coins mara mbili.
  • Lugha mbili: AppLanguage (EN/SW) ni ChangeNotifier inayotumika kila skrini.
